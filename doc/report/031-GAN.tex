\subsection{DCGAN Synthetic Dataset Generation}
\label{sec:dcgan_architecture}

A standard DCGAN architecture \cite{Radford2015DCGAN}, based on the official 
TensorFlow implementation\footnote{\url{https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials/generative/dcgan}}, 
was used to equilibrate the dataset, was used to equilibrate the
dataset, augmenting the samples of the \texttt{NORMAL} minority class.

The DCGAN architecture is composed of two adversarial neural networks:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Generator:} The network samples a 100-dimensional
  latent noise vector ($100 \times 1$) and processes it through a
  Dense layer and a Reshape operation to form an initial $8 \times
  8 \times 256$ feature map. This is followed by a sequence of
  \texttt{Conv2DTranspose} layers with fractional strides to
  upsample the feature maps to $128 \times 128 \times 1$. Batch
  Normalization and \texttt{LeakyReLU} activations are applied
  after each transposed convolution, concluding with a
  \texttt{tanh} activation function for the final image
  generation.
  
\item \textbf{Discriminator:} The discriminator evaluates the
  realism of the input images ($128 \times 128 \times 1$). It
  utilizes a series of standard \texttt{Conv2D} downsampling
  layers (filter depths of 64, 128, 256, and 512), coupled with
  \texttt{LeakyReLU} activations and Dropout regularization (rate
  of 0.3). The final layer is flattened and mapped to a single
  Dense unit that outputs a scalar real/fake score.      
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:dcgan_arch}. Both networks were compiled using Binary
Crossentropy loss and trained using Adam optimizers with a learning
rate of $10^{-4}$. The training cycle was executed over 7000 epochs.
